Part of it is the window: it slid 2 days, so 24 Sep (6 clicks) and 25 Sep (2 clicks) moved into the previous period while 22–23 Oct (1 click in total) came in, which is why clicks fell from 30 (24 Oct scan) to 23 and the change from −74.6% to −80.2%. The decline sits in the tail: 26–29 Sep had 10 clicks (~2.5 a day), while 30 Sep – 23 Oct (24 days) had only 13 (~0.54 a day). Impressions are flat (+1.6%), so visibility has not been lost — people are not clicking. Average position improved (30.9 vs 32.6) mostly thanks to brand queries ranking around 1.
