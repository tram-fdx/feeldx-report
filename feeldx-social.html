Reach has turned back up, but the two zeros are untouched. Impressions swung from −19% to +7% (1,610) and page views rose +35% (183) — the August decline has stopped. But zero comments in 30 days and zero clicks on the page's call-to-action button hold across four consecutive scans (4, 8, 9 and 10 Sep). Worse, new followers fell from 15 to 12 (−14.3%) despite a higher posting rate. Comments are what push a post beyond the follower list, and CTA clicks are what turn a viewer into a lead. More people are looking, and none of them is acting.

  • 1
    Sửa nút CTA trên trang LinkedInFix the LinkedIn page CTA button
    0 click trong 30 ngày, giảm 100% — lần thứ tư liên tiếp. Con số 0 tuyệt đối với 74 khách duy nhất (đã tăng 10%) là dấu hiệu nút hỏng, trỏ sai chỗ, hoặc dùng nhãn không ai muốn bấm. Kiểm tra nút đang trỏ đi đâu và đổi nhãn thành thứ cụ thể ("Đặt lịch tư vấn tender") thay vì chung chung. Zero clicks in 30 days, down 100% — for the fourth scan running. An absolute zero against 74 unique visitors, up 10%, points to a broken button, a wrong destination, or a label nobody wants to press. Check where it points and give it a specific label ("Book a tender consult") rather than a generic one.
