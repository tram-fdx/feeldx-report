More telling still: zero comments in 30 days and zero clicks on the page's call-to-action button hold across seven consecutive scans (4, 8, 9, 10, 11, 14 and 16 Sep). New followers are flat at 12 (0%). Comments are what push a post beyond the follower list, and CTA clicks are what turn a viewer into a lead.
