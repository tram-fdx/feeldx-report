Facebook tăng 27,6% lượt xem nhưng tiếp cận Instagram rơi 41,7%, và lượt xem IG giảm 20,7%. Tổng 2.326 lượt xem chỉ đổi được 6 follower ròng và 2 tin nhắn. Facebook is being watched more but by fewer people; Instagram has lost distribution outright. 61.4% of Facebook views and 54.3% of Instagram views come from non-followers — both down on the previous scan (63.2% and 62.6%), so the algorithm is narrowing the outward push. Facebook views rose 27.6% but Instagram reach fell 41.7% and its views fell 20.7%. The combined 2,326 views converted into 6 net followers and 2 messages. Facebook's 3-second video views fell a further 37.6% and watch time 38.9%. The constraint is no longer distribution alone — the content gives a stranger no reason to stay, and the algorithm has now started to reflect that.
