  • CTR có quay lại trên 2.5% khôngDoes CTR recover above 2.5%

A second week running below the mark: 2.98% then 2.27%. Impressions fell 36.0% while clicks fell 51.3%, so a lower CTR is arithmetically correct. But read it with position: this week has the best average position of the period (28.3) and the highest single-day CTR of the period (18 Sep, 7.2%). If CTR rebounds above 2.5% next week while impressions stay low, that is clear evidence for the "shedding junk visibility, keeping the good" reading. If CTR falls for a third week, then it is a real problem.
